For several years now, at the GMEM headquarters, I've been regularly practicing free improvisation with the musicians of Ensemble Grand 8, as well as with other guests.

Alongside Jean-Marc Montera, I'm a member of the Labo Band, in which every month we experiment with different playing practices with a dozen other musicians.

SOLO

Preferably using my Modular System or using 3 turntables + 1 extra tonearm.

Moving unique vinyl units filled with field recordings, textures which have mutated with each playing, creating an organic maturation in sound... I LOVE TO PLAY LIVE and manipulate unique vinyls with my sounds/musics, in a revolutionary way since going against the original purpose of the record. Moving it faster/slower, pushing backwards-forward, I have a physical relation to my recordings... Assaulting turntables... rubbing their stylus on paper, cardboard, metal to process unusual noises and textures…
